I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), under the supervision of [Prof. Huamin Qu](http://huamin.org/). My research focuses on creating immersive and multisensory experiences that bridge physical and digital environments, with an emphasis on Human-Computer Interaction, Interaction Design, and Virtual Reality. I am particularly interested in exploring olfactory and other sensory interactions to enrich digital experiences and enable more natural and engaging forms of interaction.

My work spans immersive system development, user-centered design, multisensory interaction, and empirical user studies. I am especially interested in how sensory feedback can be integrated with visual and interactive elements to enhance users' perception, engagement, and emotional experiences in virtual environments. Ultimately, I aim to develop intuitive and meaningful interactive systems that explore new possibilities at the intersection of human experiences and computational technologies.
